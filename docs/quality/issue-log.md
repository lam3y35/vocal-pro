| `ISS-001` | **Blocker** | Worker Inference (`engine.py` / `hardware.py`) | `engine.py` enabled `use_fp16` automatically whenever `device == "cuda"`, and `auto_optimize_for_hardware` could alter quality settings (`shifts`, `overlap`) without explicit user opt-in (violated Addendum Rule #2). | `use_fp16 = (device.type == "cuda") and bool(options.get("fp16_inference", True))` defaulted `fp16_inference` to `True`. | Changed `fp16_inference` default to `False` in `worker/vocalpro_worker/engine.py`, `src-tauri/src/worker/protocol.rs`, and `src/bindings.ts`. Made `Auto`, `Balanced`, and `Max Quality` profiles preserve `shifts=1`, `overlap=0.25`, `fp16_inference=False` on AC power. | `test_simulated_hardware_all_6_tiers_and_edge_cases`, `test_hardware_detection_all_6_tiers_battery_and_non_cuda_gpu` | **FIXED & VERIFIED** |
| `ISS-002` | **Blocker** | Rust Supervisor (`supervisor.rs` / `hardware.py`) | `WorkerSupervisor::new` defaulted `max_concurrent_workers` to `2` on any CPU with `>= 4` threads without checking available RAM, risking OOM on 8 GB RAM machines when spawning 2 Demucs worker processes (~1.8 GB RSS). | Concurrency calculation checked `cpu_threads >= 4` only, ignoring `available_ram_mb`. | Added memory-aware worker cap `((available_ram_mb - 2048) / 1200).clamp(1, 4)` in `src-tauri/src/system/mod.rs` and `worker/vocalpro_worker/hardware.py`. On this 7.9 GB RAM VM (`5.9 GB` available), `recommended_max_concurrent_jobs` is capped to `1` (`low_resource` / `<12 GB` RAM). | `test_simulated_hardware_all_6_tiers_and_edge_cases` | **FIXED & VERIFIED** |
| `ISS-003` | **Major** | Rust Audio Mixer (`src-tauri/src/audio/mixer.rs`) | Loading 6 stems × 5 minutes (`44.1 kHz` stereo) into `LiveStemMixer` decoded all samples into `Vec<f32>` in RAM, consuming **~605.6 MiB** vs the Section 13 target of **<= 150 MiB** via disk streaming. | `StemTrack` stored the entire decoded PCM stream in a single `Arc<Vec<f32>>`. | Replaced full-file in-memory buffer with a 15-second sliding disk-backed PCM window (`STREAM_WINDOW_FRAMES = 44100 * 15 = 661,500` frames per stem) with lock-free `render_interleaved_frames` and on-demand window refill (`refill_streaming_windows`). Resident buffer for 6 stems × 5 min dropped from **605.6 MiB** to **30.28 MiB** (`vm_rss_delta = 30.75 MiB`). | `test_streaming_mixer_6_stems_5_minutes_under_150mb_rss` | **FIXED & VERIFIED** |

| `ISS-007` | **Major** | Rust Worker Supervisor (`src-tauri/src/worker/supervisor.rs`) | Killing a running Python worker process mid-job with `SIGKILL` (`kill -9`) left its in-flight job waiting indefinitely instead of failing cleanly and allowing the pool to respawn on the next job. | The worker stdout reader thread exited on EOF when the child process died without signaling the `active_job` condvar or setting `status = Failed`. | Updated `supervisor.rs` stdout EOF handler to inspect `active_slot`, mark any non-terminal job as `JobStatus::Failed` (`error_code = "WORKER_CRASHED"`), and notify all waiters. | `test_multi_process_parallel_batch_and_sigkill_crash_isolation` | **FIXED & VERIFIED** |
